Legal Requirement

ESD mandates that employers demonstrate a genuine effort to hire a Malaysian before approving a foreign national for an EP. This is reviewed — not rubber-stamped. A missing or weak job ad is one of the top reasons ESD rejects post applications.

📎 Reference: MyFutureJobs Official Portal — myfuturejobs.gov.my (government job board, mandatory for ESD evidence)

Where to post — mandatory + recommended

  • MyFutureJobs — myfuturejobs.gov.my — this is the government portal ESD specifically looks for. Register as an employer, post the vacancy, keep the job ID number.
  • JobStreet — jobstreet.com.my — most widely used commercial job board in Malaysia
  • LinkedIn — optional but strengthens the evidence
  • Run the ad for a minimum of 28 days (4 weeks) before closing — shorter periods look insincere

What to write in the job ad

Post the multi-skill role clearly: "Operations Executive — property operations management, property management system (PMS), smart home/IoT coordination, digital listing management (Airbnb/Agoda/Booking.com), Canva/visual content." The combined requirement naturally limits the local applicant pool and strengthens your case.

Portal & What It Is

The ESD portal (ePASS / MYXpats Centre) at esd.imi.gov.my is the official Immigration Department platform for all Employment Pass applications. All submissions, approvals, renewals and notifications happen here — there is no paper process.

Step-by-step: create company account

  1. Go to esd.imi.gov.my
  2. Click "MYXpats Centre" → "Employer Registration"
  3. Fill in: company name, SSM registration number, business address, industry (select Services), contact person details
  4. Upload all required documents (see list below)
  5. Submit — you will receive an activation email within 3–7 working days
  6. Once activated, log in and complete your company profile before applying for any EP

Documents to upload for company registration

  • SSM Certificate of Incorporation — Form 9 (private company) or Form 13. Obtain latest copy from ssm.com.my
  • SSM Business Profile (mySSminfo) — printed within last 3 months
  • Company Tax Identification Number — from LHDN (hasil.gov.my)
  • SOCSO Employer Registration Number — from perkeso.gov.my
  • Company bank account details (for fee payments)
  • Director's MyKad (NRIC) or passport if foreign director
  • Latest audited financial statements (last 1–2 years) — shows company can afford the salary

Processing time

Company ESD profile approved within 3–7 working days. You will receive a confirmation email with login credentials.

ℹ️
If you face issues with registration, call ESD directly: +603-8000 8000 (Mon–Fri, 8am–5pm) or visit ESD One-Stop Centre, Level 1–3, Block D, Pusat Bandar Damansara, 50490 Kuala Lumpur.

To register with ESD, Maple Home must meet all of the following criteria. Tick each one before applying:

  • ✅ Registered Malaysian company (Sdn Bhd) — verified via SSM
  • ✅ Minimum 5 Malaysian employees on active payroll with SOCSO contributions (ESD checks SOCSO records)
  • ✅ Ratio of local to foreign staff — EP holders must not exceed a set percentage of total headcount. For services sector, typically no more than 1 EP per 5 local staff. With Maple Home's scale this is easily met.
  • ✅ Company not newly formed — ideally 12+ months of operations with financial records
  • ✅ Active and genuine business — not a shell company; real business premises
  • ✅ No outstanding LHDN tax penalties — ESD cross-checks with HASIL
  • ✅ Valid SSM registration — must be current, not expired/struck off

With 2,000+ rooms managed across KL and a sizeable local workforce, Maple Home easily meets all criteria.

What is an Expatriate Post?

An ESD-approved quota slot for one foreign employee in a specific role. Maple Home must justify to ESD why this particular role cannot be filled by a Malaysian. The post is approved first; then you attach the specific candidate (the Syrian worker) to the approved post in Phase 4.

Step-by-step: how to apply for the post

  1. Log into esd.imi.gov.my → MYXpats Centre → "Expatriate Post Application"
  2. Select industry: Services
  3. Enter proposed job title: Operations Executive
  4. Enter proposed monthly salary: e.g. RM 5,500
  5. Upload all supporting documents (list below)
  6. Submit — ESD officer reviews and may request additional info via email
  7. On approval you receive a Post Approval Reference Number — save this, it is needed in Phase 4

Documents to upload for post approval

  • Detailed Job Description — minimum 1 full page covering all duties, required skills, qualifications. Use Template 1 from Templates section.
  • MyFutureJobs posting screenshot — must show job title, posting date, closing date, and your company name. Download the official confirmation PDF from myfuturejobs.gov.my.
  • HR Justification Letter — signed by a company director. States why no qualified Malaysian was found. Use Template 2 from Templates section.
  • Organisational Chart — shows where "Operations Executive" sits in the company structure, who this person reports to, and who reports to them
  • Latest audited financial statements — last 1–2 years. Proves the company is financially able to pay the declared salary. Obtained from your company accountant or via SSM.
⚠️
ESD may email you requesting additional documents or clarification within the 5–15 day window. Check the registered email daily and respond within 3 working days to avoid delays.
ℹ️

Scenario 3 — No Passport At All ❌ (hardest case)

  • Request a UNHCR Attestation Letter specifically stating identity and refugee status — this serves as the primary identity document
  • Request a UNHCR Travel Document if available (issued under 1951 Refugee Convention principles, though Malaysia is not a signatory — UNHCR Malaysia may still issue this)

Step 1 — Receive the Approval Notification

ESD sends an approval notification to the employer's ePASS account. Log in, download and print the EP Approval Letter. This letter is the worker's legal authority to work while the physical EP sticker is being processed — he must carry it at all times.

Step 2 — Pay the EP Issuance Fee

Pay the issuance fee online via ePASS (included in the RM 300–600 or a separate charge — the portal will indicate the exact amount). Payment is by credit/debit card or FPX.

Step 3 — Book Immigration Appointment for EP Sticker

The EP sticker (endorsement in travel document) is placed at the nearest Immigration Department (Jabatan Imigresen Malaysia) office. Book an appointment via:

  • Online: imi.gov.my → e-services → appointment booking
  • Or call the Immigration Department: +603-8000 8000

KL Immigration offices (nearest to Maple Home operations):

  • Imigresen KL (HQ) — Jalan Duta, 50480 Kuala Lumpur. Tel: +603-6205-4422
  • Imigresen Putrajaya — Persiaran Perdana, Presint 2, 62100 Putrajaya
  • ESD One-Stop Centre — Level 1–3, Block D, Pusat Bandar Damansara, 50490 KL (handles EP-specific matters)

What to bring to the immigration appointment

  • EP Approval Letter (printed)
  • Passport or travel document (for sticker placement)
  • For Syrian with no passport: bring UNHCR card + UNHCR letter + EP approval letter. Immigration officer will advise on alternative endorsement format.
  • Original company letter confirming employment

EP sticker / endorsement processing time

Same-day or next working day at the immigration office. The worker is legally employed from the date on the Approval Letter — the sticker is the physical evidence.

Keep a certified copy of the EP Approval Letter at the Maple Home office. Under the Immigration Act, the employer must be able to produce employment documentation during any inspection.

ℹ️
After the EP is issued, there are ongoing obligations Maple Home must maintain throughout the EP validity period.
  • EP Category III: issued in 12-month validity periods
  • Apply for renewal at least 3 months before expiry via ePASS
  • At each renewal: submit an updated Succession Plan showing progress against original milestones
  • Provide updated payroll records, SOCSO, EPF contributions
  • Maximum cumulative total: 5 years — after that the role must be held by a Malaysian
  • After 5 years, if you wish to continue employing him: raise salary to RM 10,000+ and apply for Category II (no tenure cap)

Monthly obligations

  • Pay salary on time — bank transfer preferred; keep payslips every month. ESD may request 3–6 months of payslips at renewal.
  • SOCSO contributions (mandatory) — employer pays 1.75%, employee pays 0.5%, capped at RM 5,000 salary. Submit monthly via perkeso.gov.my or employer portal. EP holders fall under Employment Injury Scheme only (not Invalidity Pension). Reference: Akta Kerja 1969, SOCSO Act — perkeso.gov.my
  • EPF contributions (voluntary for EP holders) — not legally required. If offered: employer pays RM 5/month flat rate, employee pays 11% voluntarily. Reference: EPF Act 1991, Second Schedule — kwsp.gov.my
  • PCB / Monthly Tax Deduction (MTD) — deduct income tax from salary and remit to LHDN monthly via e-PCB at hasil.gov.my. EP holders are taxable residents after 182+ days in Malaysia.

Annual obligations

  • Renew EP at least 3 months before expiry via esd.imi.gov.my — upload updated payslips, succession plan progress report, and current insurance policy
  • Renew medical insurance — policy must remain valid throughout EP period
  • Submit updated succession plan progress at each renewal — show actual training completed vs. planned milestones
  • Provide 3 paid internships (1:3 policy, from January 2026) — register on myinternship.com.my (TalentCorp portal). Reference: TalentCorp Malaysia — talentcorp.com.my
  • Employee files income tax return — Form BE or M via e-Filing at hasil.gov.my

Notifications to ESD (mandatory, time-sensitive)

  • Within 14 days: notify ESD if worker resigns, is terminated, or abandons employment
  • Before any change: notify ESD if job title, salary, or role scope changes (requires a "Change of Particulars" submission on ePASS)
  • Do not allow the worker to work for any other employer — EP is employer-specific

📎 Reference: Immigration Act 1959/63, Regulation 16 (employer obligations) — imi.gov.my

What is DPP?

The Dokumen Pendaftaran Pelarian (DPP) is Malaysia's government refugee registration document, launched 1 January 2026 to replace reliance on UNHCR cards. It is managed by the Ministry of Home Affairs (MOHA) / Jabatan Imigresen Malaysia.

📎 Source: MOHA official — moha.gov.my | MOHA Facebook (official updates)

Current status as of May 2026 — 3 key facts

  • Phase 1 only: Registration is currently limited to refugees held in immigration detention centres. Community-based refugees (like someone living freely in KL) cannot register yet.
  • No community registration date announced: There is no confirmed date for when community refugees can register. Refugeemalaysia.org has explicitly warned that any announcement circulating about "Syrian/Yemeni DPP registration starting on [date]" are scams — not legitimate.
  • Work rights not yet confirmed: Even once registered, DPP may not immediately grant work rights. A February 2026 FMT article states the DPP "may not immediately confer work rights" — that framework is still being developed by the Human Resources Ministry.

Possible future benefit (if/when available)

If the Syrian worker eventually registers under DPP and work rights are granted for services sector, Maple Home would not need to sponsor an EP. That would eliminate: EP fees, succession plan, and 1:3 internship mandate. Keep monitoring for updates.

How to monitor official updates

  • Follow MOHA official Facebook page — all DPP announcements are posted here first
  • Contact UNHCR Malaysia: +603-2141-0082 or unhcr.org/my
  • Check refugeemalaysia.org for NGO updates and warnings about scams

What is IMM13?

The IMM13 is a special permit issued under Section 55(1) of the Immigration Act 1959/63, historically granted to Syrian refugees in Malaysia under the 2015 PPSMS programme. It gives the holder legal residency and the right to work.

How it helps Maple Home

If the worker already has an IMM13, Maple Home does not need to sponsor any immigration document at all. He renews the IMM13 himself annually. Maple Home just employs him with a normal contract — same as DPP, essentially no sponsorship burden.

What to do

  • Ask the worker: does he currently hold an IMM13 card? Check the physical document.
  • If yes: confirm the expiry date and renew at the nearest Immigration office before it lapses
  • Renewal fee: approximately RM 90–150/year
  • If it has lapsed: reinstatement may be possible but is discretionary — consult an immigration lawyer
⚠️
Important: IMM13 is being gradually replaced by DPP. Future renewals are not guaranteed. If he is currently on IMM13, use this option now but also start the DPP registration process in parallel as a longer-term solution.

How this works

Service apartments can apply for a foreign worker quota under the Track B / FWCMS system for roles classified as 3D (dirty, dangerous, difficult) — specifically housekeeping, cleaning, and facilities maintenance. The worker gets a Visit Pass (Temporary Employment) — VP(TE) instead of an Employment Pass.
